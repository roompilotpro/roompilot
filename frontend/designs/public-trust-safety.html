Your Safety is Our Priority

How We Keep You Safe

From verification to payments, we protect every step of your rental journey.

🛡️

Host Verification

Every host on RoomPilot undergoes thorough verification before listing properties:

  • Government-issued ID verification
  • Stripe Connect KYC compliance
  • Property ownership verification
  • Contact information validation
  • Criminal background screening available
🔍

Tenant Screening

Hosts can request comprehensive background checks and screening reports:

  • Criminal history reports
  • Eviction history searches
  • Employment verification
  • Reference checks
  • Identity verification
💳

Secure Payments

All financial transactions are processed through Stripe, the industry leader in payment security:

  • Bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption
  • PCI DSS Level 1 certified
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Never store payment info on our servers
  • Automatic payment protection
💬

Communication Safety

Keep all conversations on-platform for your protection:

  • In-app secure messaging system
  • No need to share personal contact info
  • Complete message history archived
  • Report suspicious behavior instantly
  • AI-powered scam detection
⚖️

Dispute Resolution

Fair and transparent process for resolving conflicts:

  • Dedicated support team available 24/7
  • Mediation services for disagreements
  • Documentation of all agreements
  • Security deposit protection
  • Clear terms and policies
🏠

Property Standards

We ensure all listings meet basic safety and quality standards:

  • Photo verification required
  • Accurate property descriptions
  • Working smoke and CO detectors
  • Safe living conditions
  • Compliance with local housing laws

Policy violations are taken seriously. Depending on severity, consequences range from warnings to permanent account suspension. Serious violations (fraud, discrimination, violence) result in immediate removal and potential legal action. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for scams, harassment, and illegal activity.
Background checks are optional and requested by hosts. If a host requests one, you'll receive a secure link to authorize the check. Our mediation team reviews evidence from both parties and makes a fair determination based on the lease terms and local laws.
