Getting Started

RoomPilot is a rental platform that connects hosts with people looking for affordable rooms. We offer flexible weekly rent payments, low upfront costs, and automated property management tools. Unlike traditional apartments, we focus on room rentals with month-to-month agreements and no credit checks required.
We charge significantly lower fees (2% vs 12%+ competitors), offer weekly rent collection, don't require credit checks, and give hosts full control over who lives in their property. Renters benefit from no membership fees and extremely low move-in costs.
Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner, enter your email and create a password. You'll need to verify your email address. Then complete your profile with basic information. For hosts, you'll also need to verify your identity and connect a Stripe account to receive payments.
We're currently available in most major US cities. Use the search function to see if there are listings in your area. We're rapidly expanding to new cities every month. Sign up for our newsletter to get notified when we launch in your city.

Most hosts are approved within 1-2 business days after completing identity verification and Stripe Connect setup. You can start creating your listing immediately, but it won't go live until verification is complete.

You can choose daily or weekly payouts through Stripe Connect. Funds are typically deposited 1-2 business days after rent is collected from tenants. Track all your earnings in real-time through the dashboard.
Rent is automatically charged to the tenant's payment method. If a payment fails, automatic retries occur and late fees are applied per your settings. If payment continues to fail, you can use our eviction resources and legal templates to begin the removal process according to local laws.
Yes! There's no limit to the number of properties or rooms you can manage. The dashboard gives you a unified view of all properties, tenants, and payments. Switch between properties easily to manage listings, applications, and maintenance requests.

Payments

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and ACH bank transfers through Stripe. ACH transfers are free, while card payments have a small processing fee (typically 2.9% + $0.30).
Yes! All payments are processed through Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified and uses bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store your payment information on our servers. Never send money outside the platform or via wire transfer.
Yes! If the listing offers weekly payments, you can choose to pay weekly instead of monthly. This helps budget rent if you're paid weekly or bi-weekly. Set up autopay so you never miss a payment.
You'll receive an email notification immediately. The system will automatically retry the payment over the next few days. Late fees may apply per your lease terms. Update your payment method in settings to resolve the issue and avoid additional fees.
Security deposits are held in escrow during your lease. When you move out, the host has 14 days to claim deductions with photo evidence of damages. If no claims are made or you dispute them successfully, the full deposit is returned to your original payment method.

Account

Click "Log In" then "Forgot Password". Enter your email address and we'll send you a password reset link. The link expires after 24 hours for security. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder.
Yes! One account can be used for both renting and hosting. Simply switch between "Host Mode" and "Renter Mode" in the dashboard. Your profile information stays the same, but you'll see different features depending on which mode you're in.
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. You must cancel all active leases and listings first. Once deleted, all your data is permanently removed within 30 days per GDPR requirements. This action cannot be undone.
Click your profile icon in the top right, then select "Settings". From there you can update your name, email, phone number, profile photo, and bio. Changes are saved automatically. Some changes (like email) require verification.

Trust & Safety

All hosts must verify their identity with a government-issued ID, pass Stripe KYC verification, and prove property ownership. We also verify contact information and conduct background screening when necessary.
Report it immediately using the "Report" button in any conversation or listing. Never send money outside the platform, share banking details, or wire transfer deposits. All legitimate transactions go through RoomPilot's secure payment system. Our team investigates all reports within 24 hours.
